one finding (F-03).

  - F-03 drops a source-required sentence on every path where the scheduling
    evidence is uncertain.

  `unsupported_constraints` is `[]`.

Notes:

- **F-01.** The request is unchanged, because the API has no destination
  field. But by the brief's own definition, a yes after this incomplete
  read-back is not consent, and brief line 37 forbids dispatch before consent.
  - The generated system prompt claims that the controller reads back "its
    complete final date, local HH:MM and destination as the number already
    held by the bank". That claim is false. Descriptive text is not proof.
  - Because of that claim, no model node states the destination before
    consent on the default path either.
- **F-02.** The gap fails safe: a confused answer is `unclear` and books
  nothing. It is still not the source-required dialogue.
- **F-03.** Writes stop and no close is sent, which is correct, but the caller
  hears nothing.
  - This is generic: it affects any brief that requires speech before an
    uncertain stop.
  - No lane on issue 8 owns it. The confirmation-wording fix does not cover it.
- **F-04.** The documented bank returns strings, so ordinary paths are
  unaffected. A post-process hook that records the raw value type would close
  the gap.
- **F-05.** It needs ten turns in one node visit. The stop is safe: no write
  and no claim.

## Satisfied by the sealed controller

These are runtime-controlled, not prompt text:

- **S-01:** only `schedule_callback` and `close_call` are compiled. The five
  forbidden operations have no node, and `record_notes` is false.
- **S-02:** the calendar.
  - Business days are Monday to Friday, with closures on exactly 2026-12-24
    and 2026-12-31.
  - Today is bookable even on a weekend or closure day.
  - The next business day does not count today.
  - The window 09:15-16:45 is inclusive, and a slot today must be strictly
    later than `now`'s local HH:MM.
- **S-03:** one `schedule_callback` dispatch per call (`attempt_scopes` call,
  limit 1), no retry after a definite failure, and uncertain evidence stops
  every write.
- **S-04:** closing.
  - The outcome is `TRAIN_2_O6`. R10 is sent only on recorded evidence, and
    R11 never hides a recorded callback.
  - There is one close attempt, and every close result is terminal.
- **S-05:** consent is a fresh, exact `yes` bound to the frozen request. A
  new proposal clears it, and silence, `no` or `unclear` books nothing.
- **S-06:** an absent or malformed `customer_id`, or a `now` without an
  offset, quarantines before any bank request.
- **S-07:** request fields, result fields and enum codes come from the
  OpenAPI. There are no case-ID shortcuts.

## Prompt-only items (unproven dialogue)

These depend on the dialogue model and need hosted evidence (T14):

- Turkish start and the switch to English on request, in the collection nodes.
- Brief, polite questions, and no persuasion after a decline.
- Clarifying ambiguous, ranged or incomplete proposals, and translating
  relative dates.
- Rejecting seconds and new offsets in speech. The controller sees only HH:MM.
- The different-number explanation and the existing-number question.
- Not implying verified identity.
- Acknowledging the exact recorded slot before the R10 close.
- Saying that the booking did not complete after a definite failure.
- Explaining that a recorded slot cannot be changed.
- Routing to the no-booking close when the caller stops, declines, or reports
  a wrong number or a death.
